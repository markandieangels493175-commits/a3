Privacy Policy

Clear information about how Leather Crafted Way operates.

Information we receive

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, subject and message you choose to provide. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and maintain an appropriate correspondence record.

Analytics and consent

Google Analytics is configured with consent mode. Analytics storage is denied by default and is enabled only if you choose “Allow analytics.” Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation remain denied.

We use information to operate the site, answer enquiries, maintain security and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process limited data under appropriate instructions. We retain records only as reasonably necessary.

Your choices

You may decline analytics without losing access to the site. To ask about, correct or delete personal information linked to your enquiry, email hello@leathercraftedway.com. Legal exceptions may apply.
