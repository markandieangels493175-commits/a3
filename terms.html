Using this website

This site provides original educational information about leather materials, craft planning and care. Content is general guidance, not a guarantee that a method or product will suit every item.

Material variation

Leather is a natural material. Grain, colour, temper, marks and ageing can vary. Images and sample descriptions illustrate design direction and do not guarantee an identical future object.

Responsible practice

Follow tool safety instructions, product labels, supplier specifications and the original maker’s care guidance. Test unfamiliar processes on scrap or a discreet area. Use a qualified professional for structural damage or valuable objects.
