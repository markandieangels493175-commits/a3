Shipping Policy

Clear information about how Leather Crafted Way operates.

Availability and dispatch

No product is presented as immediately available unless confirmed during an order. Dispatch estimates, carrier options and charges are provided before payment. Handmade or small-batch timelines may differ by object.

Addresses and tracking

You are responsible for providing a complete delivery address. Where tracking is available, details are sent after dispatch. Carrier scans and delivery estimates can change and are not guaranteed arrival appointments.

International delivery

International orders may incur taxes, duties, customs processing or carrier fees. Responsibility for these charges will be explained before purchase where known; border processing can extend delivery time.

Problems in transit

Inspect the parcel promptly. If it appears damaged, retain the packaging, photograph the condition and contact hello@leathercraftedway.com as soon as practical. We will review the evidence and applicable carrier process without limiting statutory rights.

Questions may be sent to hello@leathercraftedway.com or by post to 3638 Westbourne Grove, London, UK, W2 5SH, GB.
