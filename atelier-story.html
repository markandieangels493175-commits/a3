Measured work,
honest material.

Our process begins with intended use, respects natural variation and makes construction choices visible.

THE WORKING METHOD

Five checkpoints before an object leaves the bench.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Define capacity, handling, environment and the details that matter in daily use.

  2. 02

    Material

    Select a hide by structure, surface and suitability—not by colour alone.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Test access, balance, seam placement and hardware movement before final assembly.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Cut, prepare, join and finish in a sequence that supports consistency and repair access.

  5. 05

    Review

    Inspect the complete object and share care information without exaggerated longevity claims.

MATERIAL ARCHIVE

Variation is documented, not concealed.

Samples help compare weight, temper and finish under consistent light. They guide decisions; they do not erase the individual character of a hide.
